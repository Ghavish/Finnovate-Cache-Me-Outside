Scope: whole MoBudget app shell and every screen (Operate mode, code-led; no image generation). Redesign of appearance and layout only; all copy, data and features preserved. World pinned by the team's bug report: Aurora Teal palette plus inspiration screenshots (G.Take dashboard, portfolio cards, chatbot orb, wallet transactions, coming-soon glow).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Money as a calm sky at night: the app reads as one connected instrument panel lit by an aurora band, refusing the default grey admin template with purple buttons.

OWN-WORLD: Slate ground (#070B10 dark / #F4F7F8 light), raised slate panels with 1px hairline borders, teal #2DD4BF/#0D9488 as action colour, indigo #818CF8/#6366F1 as the second light. Verdict colours (mint, amber, rose) do real jobs on goal tops, badges and transaction pills. Geist type, tabular figures for every amount.

STORY: The user sees their salary, spending and goals at a glance, trusts the verdict, and reaches the next action (add data, new goal, ask coach) in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark slate rail left with new logo; breadcrumb top bar holds the section name; greeting set large left, aurora-lit hero panel with Get started; stat rail right on desktop, stacked on phone.

FORM: Operate shell, side rail + top bar (pinned by brief). seed: none (unavailable). Signature move: the aurora band, a slow teal-to-indigo light field that lives behind the hero, the coach orb and the 404 screen only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
